The problem

Finding a sitter shouldn't mean asking a Facebook group.

Irish parents pay some of the highest childcare costs in the EU. When the crèche is closed, a regular sitter cancels, or the family is new to an area, most parents fall back on word of mouth and listing sites that offer little real safety or booking support.

How it works

Like ordering a ride, but for childcare.

Payouts go to your own account, and the Uber link gets you home.

Why Aire

Safety is the product, not an add-on.

Vetted both ways

Every sitter is Garda vetted, Children First certified and first aid trained. Parents are verified too, so sitters know who they are visiting.

On demand or in advance

Find a sitter for tonight in minutes, or book one weeks ahead, the same way you'd schedule a ride.

Safe ride home

Book an Uber for the sitter from the app. Only the pick-up and drop-off details are shared, never anything about your children.

Ratings that mean something

Only parents who have booked a sitter can review them, and reviews are moderated. Sitters build a reputation that goes with them.

Private messaging

Chat stays inside the app. Phone numbers and requests to pay off the app are flagged to protect both sides.

One-tap emergency

During a booking, an emergency button calls 112/999 and alerts the parent. You can also report a concern straight to our safeguarding lead.

Questions

What does “Aire” mean?

Aire (pronounced “ar-eh”) is the Irish word for care or attention, which is exactly what we want every family and every sitter to get.

How are babysitters vetted?

Sitters verify their ID with a live selfie, complete Garda vetting through a registered route, and upload Irish Red Cross first aid and Children First certificates. An admin checks each one, and a badge is removed automatically when anything expires.

What information do you hold about my children?

Only a first name or nickname, an age bracket and optional care notes such as allergies. Care notes are visible only to the booked sitter and are deleted 30 days after the booking. We never store photos, surnames or school details of children.

How old do sitters have to be?

Sitters must be at least 16, and 16–17 year olds need a parent or guardian's consent. Sitters aged 18 or over can take bookings on their own.

Is this a real service?

Aire is a student project prototype. The app is a working demo: everything you enter is stored only in your own browser and no real payments, vetting or Uber bookings take place.
