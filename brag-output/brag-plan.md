# Aire: launch walkthrough video

**What it is:** Aire connects parents in Ireland with Garda vetted, Children First certified, first-aid trained babysitters, found in minutes.
**For:** parents who need a sitter tonight, and students or early-years workers who want safe, flexible local work.
**What sets it apart:** safety is the product. Vetting goes both ways, the family's address is only shown late, phone numbers are hidden in chat, and there's an emergency button and a safe ride home.
**Hook:** "Need a babysitter tonight?" Toy blocks drop in and stack into the Aire logo.
**Angle (as the user asked):** a full guided tour. A visible cursor drives the real site and app and shows every main feature. The video doesn't end until every feature has been shown.
**Tone:** `default`, punchy, playful and clean. Soft transitions, with the cursor and live UI supplying the motion.
**Visual identity:** the site's own colours: pink #ec4899, blue #3b82f6, purple #6b2d8f, yellow #fbbf24, on cream #fffaf3. Fonts are Fredoka and Nunito, plus the new blocks logo. Everything shown is the real site, captured live in headless Chromium.
**Format:** 1920×1080 at 30 fps. 120 BPM, so one beat is 15 frames. Every click and caption change lands on the beat grid.
**Data:** only the repo's fictional demo data (the O'Donnell family, Ellie M., Sarah K. and others). No real people's data.

## Sound
An original 120 BPM loop in C major: C – G – Am – F, one chord per bar. A soft kick on beats 1 and 3, a light clap on 2 and 4, 8th-note hats, a plucked bass, an airy pad and a marimba-style arpeggio. Cursor clicks are soft marimba ticks pitched to the current chord, mixed under the music on the same grid. The music fades in over the hook and resolves on C for the outro.
