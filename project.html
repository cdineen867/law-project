The business behind Aire

Aire was developed through the five-stage design thinking process: Empathise → Define → Ideate → Prototype → Test. From twelve early ideas, the team chose a childcare app that connects local babysitters with parents and puts safety at the centre.

The name Value proposition Market analysis Competitors SWOT Business model canvas Export potential

Why “Aire”?

Aire is the Irish word for care or attention. The name ties the app to its Irish roots and to the one thing every parent wants for their children. The logo shows three children holding hands, a picture of the trust and community the app is built on.

The tagline sums up the promise: “Babysitters you can trust, found in minutes.” It's short term, convenient, safe and on demand.

Value proposition: Safety, Convenience, Trust

“Not selling screwdrivers, selling nine-inch holes.” Parents don't want an app. They want a night out knowing their children are safe.

For parents

Find a trusted, vetted local babysitter in minutes, without relying on word of mouth or Facebook groups. Every babysitter is Garda vetted, Children First certified, first aid trained and rated by other parents. You can also book the sitter a safe ride through the Uber link.

For childminders

A safe, professional way to find regular local work, with clear ratings that build your reputation and a platform that handles bookings and payment. Parents are verified too.

Customer segments

Parents

Working parents, single parents and date-night users with young children.

New to the area

Families who have moved and have no local network, and parents whose regular sitter is unavailable.

Babysitters

Students, childcare workers and early years graduates looking for flexible, well-paid local work.

Market analysis: childcare in Ireland

Demand is high and costly to meet. Irish parents face some of the highest childcare costs in the EU, and costs for a two-earner couple are among the highest in the OECD as a share of earnings. Babysitters are a flexible, cheaper backup to crèches.

€771a month for full-time care of under-3s (2019 estimate)

Pricing logic

The average rate gives a realistic base for pricing and commission. Because every sitter's training and vetting is verified, and booking is so convenient, Aire sitters can charge a premium on top. Growth in the number of working parents is expected to keep demand for babysitting services rising.

SWOT analysis

SStrengths

  • A clear safety promise
  • A strong Irish and local angle
  • A cheap, flexible option compared with crèches

WWeaknesses

  • A new brand with no sitter pool yet
  • Vetting and insurance costs

OOpportunities

  • High childcare costs
  • Employer-benefit partnerships, as Bubble does
  • Expanding beyond babysitting to after-school cover

TThreats

  • Established Irish competitors
  • Users booking outside the app to avoid fees
  • Liability if something goes wrong

Export potential

Aire starts in Cork and Dublin, recruiting sitters through universities such as UCC. The model of a vetted on-demand marketplace with a safe ride home travels well.

  • Local first: Cork, then Dublin, Galway and Limerick, following university partnerships
  • Northern Ireland & UK: swap Garda vetting for AccessNI and DBS checks
  • EU: a GDPR-first design means a lower compliance cost in other member states
  • New services: after-school cover, and employer childcare benefits

Key issues to solve

  • Making sure vetting is legitimate and up to date
  • Safety and liability if something goes wrong
  • Building a big enough pool of sitters in each area
  • Keeping users booking through the app, not directly
  • The initial trust factor for both sitters and parents
